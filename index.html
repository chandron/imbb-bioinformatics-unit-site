Core Facility Services

How We Support Your Research

From experimental design to publication-ready figures — we assist biology labs at every stage.

RNA-seq & Transcriptomics

Bulk and single-cell RNA-seq analysis. Differential expression, GO/KEGG enrichment, pathway visualization, and publication figures using DESeq2, edgeR, and clusterProfiler.

Explore service

Genomics & Variant Calling

WGS/WES pipelines, SNP/indel detection with GATK and FreeBayes, variant annotation with VEP/SnpEff, structural variants, and population genetics.

Explore service

Training & Workshops

Hands-on workshops for wet-lab biologists: R/Bioconductor, Linux command line, reproducible analysis, and RNA-seq from scratch. Open to IMBB and University of Crete.
