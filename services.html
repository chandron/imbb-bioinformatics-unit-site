Our Services

End-to-end computational support for your biology project — from raw data to publication-ready results.

🧬

RNA-seq & Transcriptomics

Complete transcriptomic analysis from raw FASTQ files to biological interpretation. We work with both bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data.

What we deliver

Quality control reports, normalized counts, differential expression tables, volcano/MA plots, heatmaps, GO/KEGG enrichment results, and pathway visualizations.

Tools we use

STAR, HISAT2, Salmon, DESeq2, edgeR, clusterProfiler, GSEA, Seurat (scRNA-seq), and custom R/Bioconductor pipelines.

🔬

Genomics & Variant Calling

From whole-genome/exome sequencing to variant annotation and population genetics. We handle alignment, calling, filtering, and biological interpretation.

What we deliver

Alignment statistics, VCF files with annotated variants, quality-filtered call sets, variant effect predictions, and population structure analyses.

Tools we use

BWA-MEM2, GATK HaplotypeCaller, FreeBayes, DeepVariant, VEP/SnpEff, PLINK, and nf-core/sarek pipeline.

🎓

Training & Workshops

Periodic workshops and hands-on tutorials designed for wet-lab biologists who want to gain computational skills. Open to IMBB and University of Crete members.

Topics we cover

Introduction to R & Bioconductor, Linux command line basics, RNA-seq analysis from scratch, reproducible research with R Markdown, and data visualization.

Format

Half-day to full-day workshops with live coding, practice datasets, and take-home exercises. We also offer drop-in office hours for quick questions.
