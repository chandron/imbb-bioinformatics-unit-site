About the Unit

The Bioinformatics Unit is a core facility of the Institute of Molecular Biology and Biotechnology (IMBB) at the Foundation for Research and Technology - Hellas (FORTH) in Heraklion, Crete. We provide computational analysis services and training to all IMBB research groups and affiliated University of Crete laboratories.

Our Mission

To enable biology labs to extract maximum biological insight from high-throughput data through expert computational analysis, accessible self-service tools, and education. We believe every biologist should be empowered to understand and interact with their data.
