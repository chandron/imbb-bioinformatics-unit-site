💡 Not sure where to start?

If you have a list of differentially expressed genes and want to know what pathways or functions are enriched, g:Profiler or Enrichr are the fastest options — just paste your gene list and go. For ranked/pre-ranked analysis (e.g. by fold change), use GSEA. Come to our office hours if you'd like a walkthrough.
