How It Works

From Data to Discovery

Our process is designed to be straightforward. Here's what to expect:

1

Submit Request

Fill out the form below or email us. Include your experiment type, organism, and any specific questions you want answered.

2

Consultation

We schedule a brief meeting to discuss your goals, review experimental design, and agree on the analysis approach.

3

Analysis

We run the pipeline — quality control, alignment, quantification, statistical testing, and biological interpretation.

4

Results & Report

You receive a detailed report with methods section, figures, processed data files, and code for reproducibility.
